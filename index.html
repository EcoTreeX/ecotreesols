Why It Matters

The Climate Crisis in Numbers

Understanding the scale of the problem is the first step to solving it.

15B

Trees Lost Every Year

Approximately 15 billion trees are cut down globally each year — a rate that has reduced the world's tree count by nearly 46% since the start of human civilisation.

22kg

CO₂ Absorbed Per Tree / Year

A single mature tree absorbs an average of 22 kg of carbon dioxide annually. Forests collectively absorb about 2.6 billion tonnes of CO₂ per year — roughly 7% of global emissions.

10%

Emissions From Deforestation

Deforestation and land-use change account for up to 10% of global carbon emissions — making protecting and restoring forests one of the most powerful climate solutions available.

1.5°C

The Critical Warming Limit

The Paris Agreement set 1.5°C as the threshold beyond which climate impacts become severe. We are already at 1.2°C — and every fraction of a degree matters for ecosystems and communities.

80%

Of Biodiversity Lives in Forests

Forests are home to more than 80% of all terrestrial species of animals, plants, and insects. When forests disappear, so do the ecosystems millions of living things depend on.

About Eco Tree Solutions

Our Story

ECO TREE SOLUTIONS was founded with a singular, urgent belief: that the health of our planet depends on the choices we make today. Born in Nigeria, our work spans communities across Africa, where the effects of deforestation, land degradation, and climate change are most acutely felt.

We started with a simple act — planting trees. But we quickly realized that lasting environmental change requires more than seedlings in the ground. It requires community ownership, scientific rigour, policy engagement, and corporate accountability. That realization shaped everything we do.

Today, Eco Tree Solutions is a growing force for environmental action, combining grassroots energy with international-standard conservation practices to build a truly resilient, green Africa.

How It Works

Our Process

Reforestation is a science. Our meticulous 5-step approach ensures every sapling planted grows into a permanent part of the ecosystem.

Step 01

Land Assessment

We evaluate soil health, existing biodiversity, water availability, and local ecosystem needs to determine the most suitable native species to plant and identify areas with the highest restoration potential.

Step 02

Strategic Planning

Collaborating with local communities, government bodies, and environmental experts to co-design a sustainable planting blueprint that ensures community buy-in and long-term stewardship from day one.

Step 03

Active Planting

Mobilizing volunteers, community members, and trained professionals to plant native trees during optimal climate windows, using proven techniques to maximise survival rates from the start.

Step 04

Rigorous Monitoring

Tracking sapling growth, survival rates, biodiversity recovery, and carbon absorption metrics over 3–5 years with regular site visits and data reporting to stakeholders.

Step 05

Long-term Sustainability

Handing over full ecosystem stewardship to educated, economically empowered local communities — ensuring the forest becomes a self-sustaining resource for generations to come.
